How to Build a Daily Skincare Routine on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Dermio developer

Short answer. Answer a short quiz about your skin type and goals, and Dermio builds a morning and evening routine, reminds you, and tracks a daily streak. Pair it with a daily scan to see whether the routine is working.

Step by step

  1. Answer the quizTell Dermio your skin type and goal; the routine is matched to your answers.
  2. Open the Routine tabFollow the morning and evening steps and tick them off as you go (routine needs Dermio Pro).
  3. Turn on remindersGentle reminders nudge you at morning and evening times.
  4. Protect your streakDaily completion builds a streak; streak freezes cover busy days.
  5. Check the weekly reportReview the weekly skin report to see how your scores moved.

Frequently asked questions

Is the routine personalised?

It is matched to the skin type and goal you give in the quiz. It is general skincare guidance, not medical advice.

Does Dermio recommend specific brands?

The routine lists step types; the product check lets you scan an ingredient list to see what may suit your skin.

Is the routine free?

The personal routine is part of Dermio Pro.

What if I miss a day?

Streak freezes help protect your streak on busy days.

Can I use it with sensitive skin?

Dermio is not a medical tool. If your skin is reactive or you have a condition, ask a dermatologist before changing your routine.

Is Dermio a replacement for a dermatologist?

No. Dermio is for cosmetic tracking and general skincare guidance and does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. See a dermatologist for any skin concern.
