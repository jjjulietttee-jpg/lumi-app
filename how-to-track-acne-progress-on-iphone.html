How to Track Acne and Skin Progress on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Dermio developer

Short answer. Scan your face once a day in the same lighting and watch the trend, not single days. Dermio scores breakouts, pores, hydration, tone, texture and dark circles from one selfie and charts each over time, so you can see whether a new product helps.

Step by step

  1. Take your first scanComplete the short quiz, then take a selfie in even, natural light. Dermio returns an overall Skin Score from 0 to 100 and six metrics.
  2. Scan daily at the same timeRepeat in similar lighting. Scores are smoothed over time, so one odd photo does not wreck the trend.
  3. Read the Progress tabSee each metric over time, including breakouts, and compare before and after photos side by side.
  4. Change one thing at a timeWhen you add a new product, note the date and watch the trend over the next weeks.

Frequently asked questions

Can Dermio diagnose acne?

No. It tracks cosmetic changes and is not a diagnosis or a replacement for a dermatologist.

Is the free version enough to track progress?

The free tier gives one scan per day and the overall score. Per-metric breakdowns, including breakouts, need Dermio Pro.

Are my photos uploaded?

Photos are stored on your device, but each scan is sent securely to our server for analysis.

How long until I see a trend?

Skin changes slowly; give a new routine a few weeks before judging it.

Does lighting matter?

Yes. Use similar lighting each day so the trend reflects your skin, not the room.

Is Dermio a replacement for a dermatologist?

No. Dermio is for cosmetic tracking and general skincare guidance and does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. See a dermatologist for any skin concern.
