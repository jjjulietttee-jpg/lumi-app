Frequently asked questions

What is Dermio?

Dermio is an iPhone app that turns a daily selfie into a Skin Score from 0 to 100 with six metrics, builds a morning and evening routine, and tracks your progress with streaks and a weekly report.

Is Dermio free?

There is a free tier with one scan per day and the overall Skin Score. Dermio Pro unlocks all six metrics, the personal routine, product check, the assistant and weekly reports, from $9.99 per month or $39.99 per year; monthly and yearly plans include a 3-day free trial.

Does Dermio diagnose skin conditions?

No. Dermio is for cosmetic tracking and general skincare guidance. It does not diagnose or treat medical conditions and is not a replacement for a dermatologist. See a doctor for any skin concern.

What happens to my selfie?

Photos are stored locally on your device. To analyse a scan, the downscaled selfie is sent securely to our server, which returns the scores. It is not processed entirely on the phone.

Which skin metrics does it score?

Breakouts, pores, hydration, tone, texture and dark circles, plus an overall Skin Score from 0 to 100.

How often should I scan?

Once a day, in similar lighting, is enough. Scores are smoothed over time so the trend matters more than any single reading.

Can I cancel the subscription?

Yes. The subscription renews automatically unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the end of the period. Cancel any time in your App Store account settings.

Does it work for all skin types and tones?

The routine is matched to the skin type and goal you give in the quiz. Like any camera-based tool, results depend on lighting and photo quality, so treat scores as a trend indicator, not a measurement.
