How to Check Your Skin Glow Score on iPhone

Updated October 2026 · By the Dermio developer

Short answer. Take a selfie in even light and Dermio gives an overall Skin Score from 0 to 100 plus six metrics. Use it as a trend indicator: scan daily and compare weeks, not single photos.

Step by step

  1. Pick good lightFace a window or use even, soft light; avoid harsh shadows and heavy filters.
  2. Take the scanOpen Dermio, tap scan and follow the on-screen guide. It takes about 20 seconds.
  3. Read your Skin ScoreSee the overall 0 to 100 score; Pro shows all six metrics.
  4. Repeat dailyScan once a day; scores are smoothed so the trend is what matters.
  5. Review the weekly reportSee how your score and metrics changed over the week.

Frequently asked questions

Is the Skin Score a medical measurement?

No. It is a cosmetic estimate from a photo and should be read as a trend, not a clinical result.

Why did my score change overnight?

Lighting, sleep, makeup and camera angle all affect a photo. Smoothing reduces noise but does not remove it.

Is the free scan limited?

The free tier allows one scan per day and shows the overall score only.

Where does the analysis happen?

Photos are stored locally, but the selfie is sent securely to our server to be analysed.

Does it work in the dark?

Results are best in even natural light.

Is Dermio a replacement for a dermatologist?

No. Dermio is for cosmetic tracking and general skincare guidance and does not diagnose or treat medical conditions. See a dermatologist for any skin concern.
